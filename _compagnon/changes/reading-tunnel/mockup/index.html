Start from a Jira ticket

No Jira? Skip to Reviewing a plan

Your team already writes the work down in Jira. Name the ticket in your request and Claude starts the plan from it — no copy and paste, and the plan says where it comes from.

  1. You say “Prepare a plan for PROJ-123”.
  2. Claude reads the ticket once, without changing anything in Jira.
  3. You get a plan that cites it on its first line.

Example

Prepare a plan for PROJ-123

I read PROJ-123 — Dark mode for the dashboard (In Progress). The plan is in _compagnon/changes/dark-mode/: why, what changes, how, and 7 tasks. Nothing is built until you approve it.

proposal.md# Proposal: Dark mode for the dashboard

> Source: ticket **PROJ-123** — "Dark mode for the dashboard" (In Progress)
Under the hood: set it up

Compagnon needs the name of your Jira connector's ticket tool in _compagnon/config.yaml; compagnon init fills it in when it finds a Jira or Atlassian server in your Claude Code setup.

Under the hood: what Claude may do with Jira

One read of the first ticket named. Never a search, a comment or a status change.

Your first change

Already made a change? Skip to Asking Claude
