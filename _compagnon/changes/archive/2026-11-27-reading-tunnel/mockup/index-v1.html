How to read this mockup. Each card is one page of the tunnel. “Everyone sees” is the beginner layer: short text, a diagram and a concrete example. “Folded, for experts” lists what sits in closed “Under the hood” blocks. A round button lets readers skip a page that is not for them. “Next step” is where every page leads.
