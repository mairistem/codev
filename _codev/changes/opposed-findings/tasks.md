## 1. The record: the field and its checks (`codev-core`)

- [ ] 1.1 In `crates/codev-core/src/review/parse.rs` and `mod.rs`, read the optional `conflicts_with` into `ReviewFinding::conflicts_with`, and the optional `conflict_decision` (`by`, `note`) into `ReviewFinding::conflict_decision`; add both to `FINDING_FIELDS`. A `conflicts_with` value that is not a non-empty list of finding ids, an id named twice, or the finding's own id is `review_invalid` at the block. Verified by unit tests `conflicts_with_is_read_as_written`, `a_malformed_conflicts_with_is_invalid` (scenario "A malformed link": a scalar, a duplicate and a self-link, one error at each block), `a_conflict_decision_is_a_human_s_with_a_note` (scenario "A conflict decision taken by the author", `review_decision_incomplete`), `a_conflict_decision_needs_a_link` ("A conflict decision without a link"), `a_finding_without_conflicts_with_is_linked_to_none` and the existing `a_record_without_marker_is_format_one` kept.
- [ ] 1.2 Add `crates/codev-core/src/review/conflict.rs`, a pure check over the parsed `Record`. A listed id that no finding carries, and a link not listed back, are `review_invalid` at the listing finding's block, naming the other id. Call it from `parse` once the record is read, so that `codev validate` and `codev review` report it. Verified by unit tests `a_link_to_a_missing_finding_is_invalid` (scenario "A link to a finding that is not in the record"), `a_link_must_be_mutual` ("A link that is not mutual"), `mutual_links_are_valid` (the validation part of "Two opposed findings, linked both ways"), and `links_may_cross_steps`.
- [ ] 1.3 Add `Record::awaiting(&ReviewFinding)`. A finding with an unsettled link, one of whose two findings has confidence `high`, awaits `human`. A link is settled by a human's answer on either of its two findings: an entry by `human` or `human:<name>` in its history, or a `conflict_decision`. Links are judged one by one, never through a chain. Any other finding keeps `ReviewFinding::awaiting`. Verified by unit tests `a_fixed_side_and_a_withdrawn_side_await_the_human` (scenario "The author fixed one side and the other role gave way", and "A fixed finding with an unsettled link is for the human"), `a_medium_side_deferred_by_the_author_awaits_the_human` ("A medium finding deferred by the author alone"), `a_human_entry_settles_the_link` ("The human chose"), `a_conflict_decision_settles_a_link_no_entry_can_follow` ("A human's choice on a finding that takes no more entries"), `an_earlier_choice_does_not_settle_a_later_link` ("An earlier choice does not settle a later link"), `a_conflict_of_low_confidence_findings_follows_the_histories` ("A conflict of low-confidence findings"), `a_low_confidence_side_of_a_confident_conflict_awaits_the_human` ("A low-confidence finding linked to a confident one", `reported` unchanged) and `a_record_without_links_awaits_as_before` ("A record without links").
- [ ] 1.4 In `crates/codev-core/src/review/gate.rs` and `mod.rs::codes`, add the gate reason `review_conflict_unsettled`. It applies to an unsettled link joining a `high` finding with confidence `high`, whatever its status, and its message names the two linked ids and `codev review`. Verified by unit tests `an_unsettled_high_link_blocks`, `a_conflict_decision_unblocks_a_high_link`, `a_medium_conflict_does_not_block` and `a_settled_high_conflict_does_not_block`, and the existing gate tests kept.

## 3. The skills

- [ ] 3.1 In `assets/workflows/fragments/review-isolated.md`, add the meeting's **Link** bullet after "stay separate". Findings of any roles whose fixes cannot both be applied — one fix undoes or forbids the other — stay separate, and each names the other in `conflicts_with`, with the ids they are numbered with. Verified by new keywords in `propose_and_apply_describe_the_isolated_review` in `crates/codev-agents/src/workflows.rs` (scenario "The meeting rule reaches the skills"). These existing tests are kept: `a_reviewer_runs_on_its_role_s_model` ("A reviewer runs on its role's model") and `propose_and_apply_describe_the_isolated_review` ("The skills describe the isolated review"). That second test also asserts the texts behind "Two roles report the same defect" (the merge with `raised_by`), "A sub-agent fails twice" (`Fallback:`), "Material over the limit" (`summarize`, `Summarized:`) and "A change reviewed inline in an isolated project" (`inline`). The behaviors themselves need live sub-agent sessions, which no unit test can start. Scenario "Two roles ask for opposed fixes" needs a live multi-role session that the suite cannot play yet (design, evaluation decision): it is covered by that assertion and by the measure of 5.2.
- [ ] 3.2 In `assets/workflows/fragments/review-record.md`, add to step c the optional `conflicts_with: [<id>]` field and the rule that, in inline mode too, opposed findings are kept and linked. Add to step d that, while a link waits for the human (one side confident), the author fixes, disputes and defers neither finding, nor cites one to answer the other; a link between two low-confidence findings is answered as usual. Add to step f that the human's choice is recorded on the finding not chosen (`accepted-risk` or `deferred`, `by: human`) while it is `open` or `disputed`, and otherwise as a `conflict_decision` (`by`, `note`) on either finding, then the loop resumes on the chosen one. Add to step h that "To decide" presents linked findings side by side — id, role, severity, recommendation — a low-confidence one included and marked, with the choice: one fix, the other, or neither. Verified by a new unit test `propose_and_apply_link_opposed_findings` in `crates/codev-agents/src/workflows.rs` (scenario "The skills describe opposed findings"). Scenario "Opposed findings in inline mode" is covered by that assertion and by the measure of 5.2, for the same reason as 3.1. These existing tests are kept: `propose_body_carries_the_review_steps` ("The skills describe the record and the loop", and the texts behind "A maintained dispute goes to the human" and "The agent never accepts a risk"), `skills_resume_from_the_record` ("A resumed session") and `the_review_record_takes_disabled_roles_from_the_step_and_reads_codev_as_printed` ("The disabled roles come with the active ones"). The dispute and accepted-risk scenarios describe a live agent's behavior, so only their rendered rule can be asserted.
