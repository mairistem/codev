# I do not code, but I want Claude to build something for me

For you if you have an idea — a small tool, a page, a report — and want
Claude to build it, while you stay in charge of what gets built. Three
pages, about half an hour, then your first try.

1. [Compagnon in two minutes](../introduction.md)
2. [Your first change](../quickstart.md): you need Claude Code and a
   folder for your project; two commands to type once, the installation
   and `compagnon init`. If someone in your team can do them, let them.
   After that, you only talk to Claude.
3. [Reviewing a plan](../guides/review-a-plan.md): before Claude builds,
   check that its plan says what you want.
