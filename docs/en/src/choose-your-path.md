# Choose your path

You do not need to read everything. Pick the profile closest to yours and
read its pages in order; the rest of the documentation is there when you
need it.

## I am discovering AI agents

You have heard of Claude, maybe tried it, and want to see what working
with an agent looks like, safely.

1. [Compagnon in two minutes](introduction.md): what it does, who decides.
2. [Glossary](glossary.md): the words you will meet.
3. [Your first change](quickstart.md): try it on a small idea.

## I do not code, I read and approve plans

You are a product owner, a manager or a domain expert. Claude writes the
plan, you check it says the right thing, then you approve it.

1. [Compagnon in two minutes](introduction.md): the steps and your part in them.
2. [Reviewing a plan](guides/review-a-plan.md): what to check, file by file,
   and how to ask for changes.
3. [Glossary](glossary.md): proposal, spec, scenario and the rest.
