# Proposal: Birthday greetings

## Why

Members enjoy being remembered; marketing wants birthday messages.

## What Changes

- Members can opt in to birthday greetings; they then give their birthday (day and month) and a mobile phone number.
- A daily job sends a birthday text message.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `birthday-greetings`

### Modified Capabilities

None.

### Removed Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- `profiles` table, profile form, a new daily job, Textly account.
